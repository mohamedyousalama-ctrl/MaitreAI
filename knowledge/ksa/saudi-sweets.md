# حلويات سعودية (Saudi Sweets)

## Region
Kingdom-wide, with regional signatures: **kleija** and **maamoul** across Najd and the Gulf,
**debyazah** in the Hijaz, **luqaimat** everywhere in Ramadan.

## Ingredients
- **كليجا (Kleija):** spiced (cardamom, cinnamon) cookie, often **date-filled** — a Qassim/Najd icon.
- **معمول (Maamoul):** semolina/flour shortbread filled with **dates, walnuts or pistachios**, an Eid classic.
- **دبيازة (Debyazah):** a Hijazi dried-fruit-and-nut sweet paste eaten at Eid.
- **لقيمات (Luqaimat):** deep-fried dough dumplings soaked in **date syrup/honey** — the Ramadan favourite.

## Taste
Warm-spiced, buttery, date-sweet (kleija/maamoul); rich, chewy and fruity (debyazah);
crisp-outside, syrup-soaked (luqaimat). All built to go with coffee.

## Cultural significance
The sweets of **Eid, Ramadan and hospitality** — baked in big batches at home, gifted between
families, and always offered with gahwa. Maamoul especially signals Eid; luqaimat signals iftar.

## Rituals
Offered on a plate with coffee to guests; baked communally before Eid; luqaimat fried fresh at
iftar and Ramadan tents.

## Etiquette
Accept one with your coffee; complimenting the baker is expected. Odd numbers, right hand.

## Recommendation voice
- «تختم بحلا مع القهوة؟ عندنا كليجا بالتمر ولقيمات 🍯» — **only the specific sweets the tenant sells.**
- Name the exact sweet, never "we have Saudi sweets" as a blanket claim.

## Anecdote
Before Eid, the whole house smells of cardamom and dates — kids "test" the maamoul all day and
somehow the batch still lasts till the guests arrive.

## Regional variations
- **Najd/Qassim:** kleija (date-filled) is the hero.
- **Hijaz:** debyazah at Eid.
- **All:** maamoul at Eid, luqaimat in Ramadan.

## Pairings
Gahwa and Arabic tea above all; dates alongside. *(Offer only what the tenant sells.)*

## Modern context
Boxed and sold year-round, spiking at Eid and Ramadan; luqaimat is a delivery and dessert-menu
staple. These sweets combine **wheat, nuts, dairy and sometimes sesame/egg** — one of the most
allergen-dense categories, so allergy handling matters here especially.
