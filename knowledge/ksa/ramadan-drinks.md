# مشروبات رمضان (Ramadan Drinks)

## Region
Strongest in the **Hijaz** (Jeddah/Mecca iftar culture), but Ramadan drinks are Kingdom-wide.

## Ingredients
- **فيمتو (Vimto):** a concentrated berry/fruit cordial — the near-mandatory Saudi Ramadan drink.
- **سوبيا (Sobia):** a sweet, lightly-fermented **grain/oat/barley or bread**-based drink, often with cinnamon/cardamom, sometimes milky.
- **قمر الدين (Qamar al-Din):** thick **apricot-leather** juice.
- **تمر هندي (Tamr Hindi):** sweet-tart **tamarind** drink.
- **جلاب (Jallab):** date/grape molasses + rosewater, topped with **pine nuts** and raisins.

## Taste
All sweet and cooling: Vimto deep berry, sobia creamy-spiced, qamar al-din rich apricot,
tamarind sweet-sour, jallab smoky-floral. Built to rehydrate and satisfy after the fast.

## Cultural significance
Vimto on the **iftar** table is a Saudi Ramadan institution — sales spike enormously every
Ramadan and a table without it feels incomplete. Sobia carts are a Hijazi Ramadan sight.

## Rituals
Poured cold at iftar to break the fast alongside dates and water; sobia and jallab bought fresh
from Ramadan stalls in the Hijaz.

## Etiquette
Offered to all at iftar; guests served first. Sweetness is expected.

## Recommendation voice
- «تحب فيمتو بارد مع الإفطار؟ 🥤» — **only if the tenant sells it.**
- «عندنا سوبيا وقمر الدين للرمضان» — **only the specific drinks on the menu.**

## Anecdote
Come Ramadan, Saudi supermarkets stack Vimto bottles in pyramids by the door — running out
before iftar is a household emergency people genuinely plan against.

## Regional variations
- **Hijaz:** sobia and jallab stalls are a Ramadan hallmark.
- **All regions:** Vimto, qamar al-din and tamarind are universal at iftar.

## Pairings
Dates first, then Ramadan sweets, mutabbaq, harees. *(Offer only what the tenant sells.)*

## Modern context
A seasonal Ramadan hero on menus and delivery; sold by the specific drink and by size. Some
(sobia) can be grain/dairy-based and jallab carries pine nuts — relevant for gluten/dairy/nut
allergies.
