# تمر (Dates)

## Region
Universal, with the **Eastern Province — Al-Ahsa (الأحساء)** as the celebrated heartland: one
of the world's great date oases, famous for **Khalas (خلاص)** dates. **Ajwa (عجوة)** from
Madinah carries special religious esteem; **Sukkari (سكري)** from Qassim is prized for sweetness.

## Ingredients
The fruit of the date palm, eaten fresh-soft (**رطب**) or cured (**تمر**); often **stuffed
with nuts, orange peel, or filled/coated** in modern gift boxes.

## Taste
Ranges from the light, crisp-sweet **Sukkari** to the soft, caramel-rich **Khalas** and the
dark, dense **Ajwa**. Natural candy — deeply sweet, sometimes with notes of caramel or honey.

## Cultural significance
Inseparable from **gahwa** — «قهوة وتمر» *is* Saudi hospitality. The Sunnah of breaking the
**Ramadan** fast with dates makes them sacred to the season. A staple of welcome, gifting and
generosity across every region.

## Rituals
Offered with coffee to every guest; the first thing eaten at iftar (odd number, by tradition);
presented in fine boxes as gifts for Eid, weddings and visits.

## Etiquette
Take with the **right hand**; accepting a date offered with coffee is basic courtesy. Refusing
outright can read as cold — a polite "بعد القهوة" softens it.

## Recommendation voice
- «تحب تمر مع القهوة؟ عندنا سكري خفيف وخلاص طري 🌰» — **only the varieties the tenant sells.**
- «تختم طلبك بتمر؟ حلو طبيعي يمشي مع الشاي.» — offer only if dates are on the menu.

## Anecdote
Ahsawis will argue that a Khalas date at the perfect **رطب** stage — halfway between fresh and
cured — is the finest bite in Arabia. Season it right and you'll be believed.

## Regional variations
- **Al-Ahsa (Eastern):** Khalas — the oasis benchmark.
- **Madinah (Hijaz):** Ajwa — religious esteem.
- **Qassim (Najd):** Sukkari — light and very sweet.

## Pairings
Gahwa first; Arabic tea; laban. Often stuffed with nuts or paired with Saudi sweets. *(Offer
only what the tenant sells.)*

## Modern context
Sold loose, boxed, stuffed, chocolate-covered, and as date syrup (دبس); a year-round gift and
a Ramadan essential. Because dates are so often **stuffed with nuts**, they're a real allergen
touchpoint.
