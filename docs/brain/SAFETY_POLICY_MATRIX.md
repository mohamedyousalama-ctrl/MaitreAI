# BRAIN Safety Policy Matrix

The model may add evidence and natural language, but it may never authorize "safe", use "آمن" as reassurance, or override a deterministic positive safety detector.

| scenario | detector trigger | forced response blocks | kitchen-note behavior | human-escalation behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ingredient info request | Customer asks what is inside an item, asks about ingredients, or asks whether an ingredient is present without stating allergy/health risk. | Provide ingredient facts from catalog/evidence only; state uncertainty when catalog lacks detail; ask whether there is an allergy or dietary constraint if risk is plausible. | No kitchen safety note unless customer discloses allergy/health risk. | Escalate if catalog evidence is missing and the answer would affect safety or compliance. |
| Explicit allergy while ordering | Customer states allergy, sensitivity, celiac/gluten issue, medical restriction, or severe reaction while selecting items. | Acknowledge the disclosure; do not authorize safety; ask clarifying question or offer human confirmation when needed; block commit until safety disclosure is structured. | Create/update `safety_disclosures`; require kitchen-visible note on any committed order. | Escalate when item ingredients, prep process, or cross-contamination status is unknown or customer asks for safety assurance. |
| Unknown ingredient or cross-contamination | Catalog lacks ingredient/prep evidence, or cross-contact is unknown for a disclosed safety constraint. | State that the system cannot confirm suitability; offer alternate items only from verified evidence; avoid reassurance. | Mark disclosure as requiring kitchen confirmation; include unknown/cross-contamination concern if order proceeds after human handling. | Human handoff is required before commit if the customer wants to proceed on the risky item. |
| Low-confidence voice plus possible allergy | ASR confidence is low and transcript contains possible allergy, health, ingredient, or symptom term. | Treat as safety-possible; ask customer to confirm the term in text or clear voice; do not proceed to commit on the ambiguous item. | No final kitchen note until confirmed, but keep trace as safety evidence for the episode. | Escalate if repeated low-confidence turns still suggest allergy/health terms or customer attempts confirmation. |
| Historical allergy memory | Explicit reconfirmed safety disclosure exists from prior confirmed interaction. | Surface a deterministic reminder and ask for reconfirmation for the current order; do not silently apply inferred safety. | Kitchen note only after reconfirmation in the current episode or explicit standing consent policy. | Escalate if reconfirmation conflicts with current item, catalog evidence, or customer says the memory is wrong. |
