1. Executive Snapshot
The photo in the query is Al Wattan Medical Complex 1 (مجمع الوطن الطبي 1), a 24-hour ambulatory hospital / polyclinic at 2807 Prince Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Road, Al Yamamah (also described locally as Atika / عتيقة, east of Manfuha), Riyadh 12671. It is the founding site of Al Wattan Medical Group, established in 1982.
The parent is a closed joint-stock company registered with the Saudi Ministry of Commerce (registered as a group in 2000). It owns and manages six ambulatory healthcare complexes in Riyadh only — no confirmed branches outside Riyadh were found under this exact brand. It also owns/manages the Diyar Pharmacy chain and offers pre-employment medical screening for companies and government agencies.

Accreditation

CCHI / insurance-network facility codes appearing in insurer PDFs include 18002 (Complex 1), 18003 (Complex 2), 17985 (Complex 3), 18004 (Shoaa 1). Shoaa 2 appears under older codes such as 17081 in some maps.
History timeline
1982 — Al Wattan Medical Complex 1 opens in Al Yamamah / Manfuha–Atika area.
1992 — Shoaa Medical Complex founded on King Abdullah / Prince Abdullah Road, Al Wurud.
1999 — Al Wattan Medical Complex 2 opens on Unayzah Street, Ar Rawabi.
2000 — Leadership registers Al Wattan Medical Group; Shoaa joins the group and is later renovated.
2022 — Group acquires Complex 3 (Ar Rabwah, previously referenced as Modern Medical Center in some insurer lists) and Shoaa Medical Complex 2 (Ar Rawdah; previously Mashfa / المشفي medical center).
13 March 2023 — Shoaa Al Wurud granted CBAHI accreditation.
2023 — Group acquires Complex 4 in Ash Shifa (Ibn Tulun / Ibn Taymiyyah corner). Official website pages from 2023 still labelled it “Temporarily Closed”; by June 2024 the group announced Bupa patients were being received there, and by July 2026 the official X account was advertising orthodontic offers at the Shifa branch — treat as operational unless a call says otherwise.

4. How to Book and Pay
Walk in at any branch reception.
Call the branch landline or the published unified numbers 920009303 (Watan group directories) / 920002258 (Shoaa).
WhatsApp +966 50 449 0460 (repeated on both brands).

Cash-pay consultation fees seen on Tebcan for Shoaa doctors typically fall in the 56–200 SAR range depending on specialty (listed as “free consultation” promotions that exclude procedures). These are marketplace figures, not official tariffs.
Insurance and TPA presence
The complexes appear on multiple Saudi health-insurance provider networks (MPN / OCN / OHN class lists). Documented appearances include Tawuniya-class hospital lists, Bupa Arabia (explicit 2024 announcement for Complex 4; Shoaa directories historically list Bupa among accepted insurers), Medgulf, Walaa, Gulf General, SAICO, Malath, United, Al-Etihad, Enaya Saudi, Solidarity, and Takaful Al Arabia / Takaful Watan discount cards. Always verify the current network class (A/B/C) and deductible on the patient’s card — published discount percentages (20–30% on consults, labs, dental, laser) apply to specific TPA cards and exclude some campaign days.

7. What This Group Is — and Is Not
It IS a multi-site private ambulatory network concentrated in Riyadh, with a 40+ year operating history at the Yamamah site.
It IS the owner of the Shoaa brand; searching only “الوطن” misses two of the six sites.
It is NOT Al Watani Hospital / المستشفى الوطني (different facility, different phone 011 437 7777) and NOT “المجمع الوطني الطبي” in Qurayyat. Name collision is common in Arabic search.
It is NOT a Ministry of Health hospital and not a large tertiary inpatient centre. Think polyclinic + ER + day surgery + pharmacy.
No evidence was found of branches in Jeddah, Dammam or other cities under this exact group. One unrelated “Watan” dental listing in Taif appeared in a third-party number dump and should not be assumed to be the same company.
8. Practical Visit Notes for Complex 1 (the photographed site)
Expect a multi-storey white building with a red fascia, surface parking, and a lobby ATM (SABB) as in the listing photos.
Call 011 458 8444 or WhatsApp 050 449 0460 before arriving on Friday; hours compress that day.
Bring national ID / Iqama and insurance card. Ask reception which network class the visit will bill under.
If the goal is dermatology/laser or a specific dentist, Complex 2 (Rawabi) is the branch the group markets most heavily for those services.
If the goal is CBAHI-accredited ambulatory care or the Shoaa app experience, use Shoaa Al Wurud.
For Shifa / south Riyadh, call Complex 4 first — website vs. social status has disagreed since 2023–2024.
