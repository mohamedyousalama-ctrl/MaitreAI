MaitreAI — 5 design directions

An AI employee for restaurants in Egypt & Saudi — running WhatsApp ordering, promotions & CRM, supervised by non-technical owners and floor staff. Five ways to collapse a 10-section admin panel into something a busy manager checks in 15 seconds, one-handed. Screens are Arabic-first (RTL); annotations are in English.

AI / agent Urgent escalation · SLA Text placeholder العربية · RTL
1

Layer — WhatsApp-native minimal

A thin power-layer over the chats your staff already live in. It feels like WhatsApp, not a SaaS panel.

Philosophy

Don't make staff learn a new tool. Wrap the chat surface they know in a quiet supervision layer — agent tags, takeover, status — and hide everything else.

Information architecture · 10 → 3
المحادثات الطلبات المزيد

3 bottom tabs. القائمة · العروض · العملاء · عقل المطعم · الأرقام all fold into More. Open/Closed & AI-pause sit in a pinned banner atop the chat list; escalations are pinned threads.

Handover

Inside a thread: AI bubbles are amber-tagged, a copilot draft sits above the composer, and one big «تولّى المحادثة» button transfers control mid-conversation.

+Familiar, fast, one-thumb. Near-zero training cost.
−No at-a-glance numbers; analytics & bulk menu work buried in More.

2

Pulse — operations cockpit

Walk past the till and know the restaurant's state in one glance. Big tiles, big numbers, voice for hands-free.

Philosophy

Supervision-at-a-distance. Status is the product; everything else is a tap away. Glanceable from across a noisy room, readable on the cashier screen too.

Information architecture · 10 → board + 2
اللوحة المحادثات الطلبات

The Board is home: live tiles for numbers, escalations, AI & Open/Closed. القائمة · العروض · العملاء · عقل المطعم live behind a gear. Voice / glance folded in — a mic does any command hands-free.

Handover

A red escalations tile opens a focused takeover with an SLA countdown ring; the AI's draft is a big accept/edit card — decide in two taps.

+Instant situational awareness; doubles as a till dashboard.
−Less thumb-friendly — tiles scatter focus; chat depth is one level down.

3

Ask — AI-first command bar

Stop navigating — tell your AI what to do. A persistent command bar runs the whole restaurant.

Philosophy

The UI is a sentence. Type or speak intent in dialect — «اعمل عرض ٢٠٪», «أرقام اليوم» — and the answer or action renders. Screens become results.

Information architecture · 10 → 0 + bar
⌘ اكتب أمر…

No tab bar. The command bar is the nav; sections collapse into intents that return result cards. Suggestion chips seed discovery; Open/Closed & AI-pause are persistent top toggles.

Handover

Ask «التصعيدات» → escalation cards with SLA. Each carries an AI draft you send, or tap «تولّى» to control — all from the bar.

+Ruthlessly minimal; infinitely extensible; fastest for power users.
−Blank-canvas discoverability; leans hard on dialect NLU; steeper for day-one staff.

4

Stream — single triage feed

One prioritized stream of everything that needs a human. Clear the feed, the restaurant runs itself.

Philosophy

Zero-decision navigation. One feed mixes every item type that needs you — escalations, new orders, stock flags, promo results — ranked by urgency. Swipe to act or dismiss.

Information architecture · 10 → 2
التدفّق المطعم

Just 2 tabs. Stream is the triage feed; المطعم holds everything you go to deliberately — orders, menu, promos, customers, brain, numbers. Open/Closed & AI-pause pin to the header.

Handover

Escalations are feed cards: snippet + AI draft + «تولّى» inline. Tap to expand the full thread without leaving the stream.

+Perfect for 10-second checks — "what needs me right now?" answered instantly.
−No spatial memory — items move & vanish; deliberate bulk work feels secondary.

5

Maître — AI colleague console

Manage your restaurant by managing one employee. The whole app is a conversation with your AI maître d'.

Philosophy

Lowest cognitive load: you don't operate software, you supervise a competent colleague. It reports proactively in dialect; you reply. Actions arrive as rich cards inside the thread.

Information architecture · 10 → 1 thread
محادثة المساعد + tools

One 1:1 thread with the AI. Sections become summoned cards (orders, promos, customers) inside it; a slim state strip pins Open/Closed · AI · today's number, and «+» opens structured tools for deliberate edits.

Handover

The AI posts an escalation card — «منى زعلانة من التأخير، أرد إزاي؟» — with a draft. Tap send, or «أنا أردّ» slides the real customer thread up as an overlay.

+Delightful, differentiated, near-zero learning — the "AI employee" promise made literal.
−Linear & low-density — hard to scan many items; rote bulk tasks feel slow.
