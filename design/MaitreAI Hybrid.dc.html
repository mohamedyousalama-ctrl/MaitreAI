Why a pipeline, not a board

Staff never see a kitchen-board screen. The phone is the board: four glanceable stages, one swipe to advance.

New — just landed from WhatsApp
Preparing — ticket auto-printed
Ready — awaiting pickup / rider
Out — on the way to the customer

Print & reprint live on every card and in the detail — for the thermal kitchen printer, including a torn-ticket reprint.
